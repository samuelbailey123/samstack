# Sharpen

Make the task deeper, not bigger, then rewrite the ask. Adapted from pstack's sharpen ([cursor/plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins)), MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Lauren Tan: see `LICENSE`.

When the user typed `/samstack:sharpen`, give them the four answers and the rewritten ask. Otherwise the rewrite is your own brief, and the user sees one line of it: `Reading this as: <the deeper ask>`.

## The method

Before rewriting the ask, do four things:

1. Say what a top professional in this field would do here that the ask leaves out.
2. Name the rawest material they would start from instead of what has already been digested.
3. Name the specific methods and tools they would use.
4. Say what, other than you or the user, would show the result is right.

Then rewrite the ask to call for exactly that. The rewrite prototypes any choice an experiment can settle and recommends an option based on what it observed. It leaves product or preference choices that no experiment can settle to the user.

## When it fired on its own

1. Work the four answers for yourself. The rewritten ask is the brief you work from.
2. Before any other tool call, write the user one line: `Reading this as: <the deeper ask>`. After a reframe, that is the reframe's line: one line for both.
3. Do the work. Prototype each choice an experiment can settle, and recommend from what you observed. A product or preference choice that changes the result is the one question you ask, your pick first.
4. The four answers go in the work's ticket or note, or in a spec kept out of the repo.
5. Your final reply opens with the answer. The `Reading this as:` line is the one you wrote before the first tool call; do not repeat it.
